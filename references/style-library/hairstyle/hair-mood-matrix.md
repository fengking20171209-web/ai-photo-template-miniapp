## 关键情绪规律

1. **confident 自信**：wet-wave, high-ponytail, low-bun, cyber-silver — 能传递掌控感的发型
2. **mysterious 神秘**：center-straight, low-bun, oriental-bun — 有遮挡或古典感的发型
3. **playful 俏皮**：high-ponytail, bun-clean, twin-ponytails — 有弹跳感的高扎发型
4. **energetic 活力**：high-ponytail, bun-clean, twin-ponytails — 运动感强的发型
5. **luxurious 奢华**：princess-half, low-bun, french-curl, oriental-bun — 精致复杂的盘/卷发
6. **dominant 主导**：low-bun, cyber-silver, cyber-wet — 有力量感和距离感的发型
7. **editorial 编辑**：几乎所有发型都可，重点是造型的精致度和概念感
8. **cold 冷酷**：cyber-silver, cyber-wet, center-straight — 冷感、距离感的发型
