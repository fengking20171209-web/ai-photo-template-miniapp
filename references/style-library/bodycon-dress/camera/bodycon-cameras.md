## 85mm 全身（最稳）

```
85mm lens, full-body composition,
natural body proportions, no distortion,
shallow depth of field, subject sharply in focus,
editorial fashion photography
```

**适合**：所有轮廓，比例最自然

---

## 70mm 时装感

```
70mm lens, three-quarter to full body,
slight compression enhancing silhouette,
fashion editorial framing,
subject filling 60-70% of frame
```

**适合**：展示服装结构细节

---

## 50mm 全身（居家/自然）

```
50mm lens, full-body composition,
natural perspective closest to human eye,
intimate residential or hotel setting,
warm natural light
```

**适合**：酒店场景·居家·日常

---

## Low Angle 全身（拉长腿线）

```
low angle full-body shot,
camera at hip height looking slightly upward,
leg line elongated proportionally,
NOT extreme, subtle 15-degree tilt
```

**适合**：展示腿部比例，但不能过度

---

## Clean Front View（展示裙型）

```
straight-on front view,
full-body composition,
symmetrical framing,
dress silhouette clearly visible,
editorial catalog style
```

**适合**：展示裙子整体轮廓

---

## Three-Quarter View（展示腰臀线）

```
three-quarter angle view,
body slightly turned,
waist-to-hip curve accentuated,
natural weight shift visible,
flattering body line composition
```

**适合**：展示bodycon的曲线效果

---

## 避免的镜头

```
extreme wide angle, fisheye lens,
overly close body crop, first-person angle,
extreme low angle distorting proportions
```
