## 01. Spaghetti Strap Bodycon — 细肩带经典款

**编码**：spaghetti_strap_bodycon
**核心特征**：细肩带 + 方圆领口 + 哑光贴合

```
black spaghetti strap bodycon mini dress,
clean scoop neckline,
thin shoulder straps,
matte compact stretch jersey fabric,
soft sculpted silhouette,
subtle waist shaping,
smooth hip line,
short fitted hem with natural fabric tension
```

**适合场景**：白色摄影棚·极简酒店·都市公寓·黑白杂志
**搭配**：裸腿或裸感丝袜·细高跟·极简配饰
**情绪**：editorial + confident
**镜头**：85mm全身
