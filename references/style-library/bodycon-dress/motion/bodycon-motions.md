# BodyconDressDNA — 动作数据库 v1.0

> 版本：v1.0 | 创建：2026-05-28 | 5 个bodycon专用动作
> 核心原则：微动态 + 体态线条，不能太夸张否则僵硬

---

## 1. SLIGHT_FORWARD_LEAN — 轻微前倾

```
slight forward lean,
one shoulder relaxed,
natural waist curve visible,
hands gently adjusting the hem or resting at side,
editorial calm expression,
subtle fabric tension at the hip
```

**适合轮廓**：全部
**适合情绪**：candid + elegant
**镜头**：85mm半身

---

## 2. THREE_QUARTER_TURN — 侧身回头

```
three-quarter side pose,
head turned toward camera,
one hand resting near waist,
natural weight shift to back leg,
smooth hip line accentuated,
S-curve posture from head to toe
```

**适合轮廓**：全部（尤其展示腰臀线）
**适合情绪**：confident + mysterious
**镜头**：85mm全身

---

## 3. WALL_SUPPORT — 扶墙站姿

```
standing beside a clean white wall,
one hand lightly touching the wall surface,
one leg slightly forward,
long leg line emphasized,
relaxed elegant posture,
fabric draping naturally against the wall
```

**适合轮廓**：spaghetti_strap + ribbed_knit
**适合情绪**：casual + confident
**镜头**：50mm全身

---

## 4. WALKING_FORWARD — 慢步走来

```
walking slowly toward camera,
one leg crossing naturally in front,
subtle fabric tension at the hip with each step,
candid editorial movement,
natural arm swing,
slight motion in the hair
```

**适合轮廓**：ruched_side + bandage
**适合情绪**：candid + editorial
**镜头**：85mm全身 + 35mm low angle

---

## 5. SEATED_CROSSED — 坐姿交腿

```
seated on modern lounge chair or bench,
legs crossed elegantly,
straight back, shoulders relaxed,
soft confident expression,
fabric bunching naturally at the lap,
minimalist fashion portrait composition
```

**适合轮廓**：ribbed_knit + spaghetti_strap
**适合情绪**：editorial + sophisticated
**镜头**：50mm半身到全身
