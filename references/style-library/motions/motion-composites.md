## SIUF_RUNWAY_WALK — T台步态

> 最核心的 SIUF 动作，用于所有走秀场景

### 组合分解
基于：RUNWAY_WALK + EDITORIAL_STAND

### 执行序列
1. 正面走来，步态稳健有力
2. 定点pose切换，侧身展示
3. 回眸收尾，冷峻表情

### HEAD
head_high, gaze_forward→camera, chin_level, expression_fierce→knowing

### TORSO
torso_upright_dynamic, shoulders_back, core_engaged, slight_twist_at_pose

### HIP
hip_sway_confident, weight_shifting_rhythmically, pelvic_forward

### LEGS
long_stride, toes_pointed, heels_high, runway_gait

### HANDS
arms_swinging_naturally→hand_on_hip→touching_hair

### 动态修饰
walking, commanding, rhythmic, fashion_show

### 场景适配
情绪：confident + dominant
服装：lace + silk + sportswear（全品类）
镜头：35mm全身 + low_angle + 135mm长焦
灯光：runway_spotlight

## SIUF_LOOKBACK_BEND — 回头弯腰

> 经典性感动作，回眸+弯腰的组合，S曲线极致展现

### 组合分解
基于：LOOKBACK_BEND + TWIST_TURN

### 执行序列
1. 背对镜头，弯腰前倾
2. 回头看镜头，臀部后翘
3. 一手扶腿或扶墙，定格

### HEAD
head_turned_180deg, gaze_camera, chin_tilt_down_10deg, expression_sultry

### TORSO
torso_bent_forward_25deg, twist_45deg, lumbar_exaggerated, back_arched

### HIP
pelvis_tilt_back, hip_pushed_back_extreme, weight_shift_right, S_curve_maximized

### LEGS
one_leg_forward_straight, weight_on_back_leg, knee_bent, hip_jutted

### HANDS
one_hand_on_thigh, other_hand_near_face or on_wall, supporting

### 动态修饰
dynamic, caught_mid_bend, dramatic, seductive

### 场景适配
情绪：confident + mysterious
服装：lace + silk
镜头：low_angle + 85mm + 50mm
灯光：runway_spotlight + neon_edge

## SIUF_POOLSIDE_RECLINE — 泳池边侧卧

> 泳装专用，慵懒奢华的水边姿态

### 组合分解
基于：POOLSIDE_RECLINE + FLOOR_POSE

### 执行序列
1. 侧卧在泳池边躺椅或地面
2. 上身微起，手撑头部
3. 双腿优雅交叠或伸展

### HEAD
head_resting_on_hand, gaze_camera_or_sky, chin_up_10deg, expression_relaxed_sultry

### TORSO
torso_lateral_bend, reclined_45-60deg, ribcage_open, relaxed

### HIP
pelvis_shifted, hip_pushed_out, weight_on_side, curve_accentuated

### LEGS
legs_extended_elegant, crossed_at_ankle, or_one_bent, toes_pointed

### HANDS
one_arm_supporting_head, other_on_thigh_or_near_face, relaxed_fingers

### 动态修饰
languid, poolside, luxurious, relaxed_confidence

### 场景适配
情绪：playful + confident
服装：swimwear（全品类泳装）
镜头：low_angle + 35mm全身 + 85mm半身
灯光：golden_hour + pool_reflection

## SIUF_EDITORIAL_STAND — 杂志定点

> 杂志封面级别的定点pose，冷静大气

### 组合分解
基于：EDITORIAL_STAND + SIDE_POSE

### 执行序列
1. 正面或四分之三侧面站立
2. 重心一脚，胯部微偏
3. 表情冷静，下巴微抬

### HEAD
head_neutral_or_tilt_back_5deg, gaze_camera_intense, jaw_defined, expression_editorial

### TORSO
torso_upright_proud, shoulders_back, chest_expanded, silhouette_defined

### HIP
pelvis_anterior_tilt, hip_shift_one_side, weight_on_back_leg, contrapposto

### LEGS
contrapposto_stance, one_leg_straight, one_leg_relaxed_bent, weight_shifted

### HANDS
one_hand_on_hip, other_relaxed_or_touching_hair, fingers_elegant

### 动态修饰
powerful, editorial, still, commanding, haute_couture

### 场景适配
情绪：editorial + dominant + cold
服装：全品类（尤其 cyberwear + lace）
镜头：50mm半身 + 35mm全身 + 135mm长焦
灯光：soft_editorial_box + cinematic_tungsten

## SIUF_CANDID_MOTION — 抓拍动态

> 自然抓拍感，不经意间的美，最考验"演"的自然度

### 组合分解
基于：CANDID_MOTION + HAIR_FLIP + WALKING

### 执行序列
1. 自然行走或转身
2. 整理头发或调整服装
3. 抓拍瞬间的自然表情

### HEAD
head_turned_away→camera, gaze_averted→soft_gaze, expression_natural, smile_or_neutral

### TORSO
torso_natural_twist, unposed, body_in_transition, slight_momentum

### HIP
hip_natural_shift, weight_in_motion, walking_dynamic

### LEGS
mid_step, natural_gait, one_foot_ahead, weight_transfer

### HANDS
adjusting_hair, touching_neck, holding_bag, gesturing_naturally

### 动态修饰
candid, spontaneous, unposed, natural_light, motion_blur_slight

### 场景适配
情绪：candid + playful + energetic
服装：全品类（最配 sportswear + lace）
镜头：50mm + 85mm + 35mm
灯光：golden_hour + soft_editorial_box
