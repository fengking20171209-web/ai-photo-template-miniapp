## 五、场景 × 完整搭配方案

### 5.1 职场/商务

```
high-waisted tailored pencil skirt in dark navy or charcoal,
structured wool-blend fabric, above-knee length,
paired with crisp white button blouse,
pointed-toe pumps in black or nude,
minimal gold jewelry, structured handbag
```

### 5.2 晚宴/酒会

```
high-waisted satin bodycon mini skirt in black or deep burgundy,
lustrous fabric with subtle sheen,
paired with silk camisole or delicate lace top,
strappy heeled sandals, statement earrings,
clutch bag, elegant updo hairstyle
```

### 5.3 街拍/日常

```
high-waisted denim mini skirt or stretch knit bodycon,
raw hem or distressed detail,
paired with oversized graphic tee or crop top,
sneakers or ankle boots, crossbody bag,
casual confident posture
```

### 5.4 夜店/派对

```
high-waisted faux leather or patent PVC mini skirt,
semi-gloss or high-gloss finish,
paired with sequin crop top or mesh bodysuit,
platform heels, bold statement jewelry,
dramatic makeup, confident stance
```

### 5.5 杂志大片

```
designer bodycon pencil skirt in unique fabric or color,
architectural details, asymmetric hemline,
paired with editorial conceptual top,
styled with purpose, professional studio lighting
```

### 5.6 新中式

```
high-waisted satin mini skirt in deep emerald or cream,
soft fitted silhouette, curved hemline,
paired with dudou-inspired halter top,
pointed-toe heels, traditional fan prop,
oriental hotel interior backdrop
```

### 5.7 运动休闲

```
high-waisted stretch knit bodycon skirt,
performance fabric with matte finish,
paired with sports bra or cropped hoodie,
chunky sneakers, sporty accessories
```

## 七、系统搭配思维（来自时装工业研究）

### 核心原则

> 高级的紧身包臀，并不是把所有位置都收得更紧，而是决定哪里要被"托住"，哪里要被"放松"，哪里要有光，哪里要哑光，哪里要被丝袜和鞋子连成一条线。

### 公式1：90年代极简（最稳）

`
basic top + sleek miniskirt
简单上衣（白T/针织背心/干净衬衫）+ 干净包臀裙
`

### 公式2：上宽下窄对比（最有时装感）

`
oversized blazer / oversized shirt + bodycon mini
上半身有结构 → 下半身的贴合看起来是时装不是"紧"
`

### 公式3：Saint Laurent式权力搭配

`
leather pencil + crisp blouse with bow
下半身收 + 上半身放 + sheer stockings连接 + pointy pump收尾
`

### 公式4：材质升级三步走

`
1. 裙体：单层薄针织 → compact jersey / double-knit / matte bandage
2. 丝袜：厚黑 → 8-20D 烟灰/肤色/tone-on-tone
3. 外层：无 → 短西装/干净衬衫 → 制造上宽下窄对比
`

### 丝袜正确用法

| 场景 | 选择 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| 高级轻盈 | 8-20D 肤色/烟灰/淡咖/石墨灰 | 半透低存在感 |
| 存在感强 | 黑色/有图案 | 需要上半身也足够重 |
| 不要 | 厚黑+简单吊带裙 | 切断感+廉价感 |
