## 一、8 种结构变体

### 01. CLASSIC_PENCIL — 经典铅笔裙

**编码**：classic-pencil
**腰线**：高腰 | **长度**：及膝或膝上
**结构**：前后两片式，侧缝直线，下摆微收

```
high-waisted classic pencil skirt,
fitted silhouette with straight side seams,
slightly tapered hemline, structured tailoring,
clean minimal lines, knee-length or just above
```

**最佳场景**：职场·知性·正式
**最佳面料**：西装面料·弹力针织

## 六、常见AI畸变与对策

| 畸变 | 原因 | 对策 |
|------|------|------|
| 裙子变裤子 | 模型混淆 | 加 `skirt NOT pants NOT shorts NOT trousers` |
| 裙摆过长 | 位置不准 | 加 `above-knee, mini length, upper thigh hemline` |
| 腰线错位 | 腰线模糊 | 加 `high-waisted at natural waistline, navel-level` |
| 面料质感丢失 | 描述不足 | 加具体面料词 `stretch satin / knit jersey / faux leather` |
| 臀部比例失调 | 过度强调 | 加 `natural hip curve, balanced proportion, realistic body` |
| 裙子飘起 | 动态干扰 | 加 `stationary fabric, gravity-appropriate drape` |
| 褶裥消失 | 细节不足 | 加 `sharp knife pleats, crisp folded fabric` |

## 七、4种核心轮廓（来自时装工业研究）

### 轮廓A：Bandage Bodycon（绷带塑形）

`
bandage construction dress,
horizontal sculpting bands wrapping the body,
firm supportive structure, contour-and-define logic,
body-contouring through bust waist and hip,
NOT thin single-layer stretch, structured panels
`

最佳面料：bandage knit, heavy elastomeric
最佳场景：晚宴·红毯·编辑大片
注意：光泽不能太高，分割线不能太碎，避免回到2000s夜场审美

### 轮廓B：Draped / Ruched Body-skimming（垂坠贴身）

`
draped fabric falling naturally over the body,
ruched gathering at side seams,
body-skimming not body-tight silhouette,
soft movement with every step,
NOT rigid or compressed, fluid ease
`

最佳面料：fluid jersey, matte satin, silk charmeuse
最佳场景：从白天到夜晚的转换场景
关键：侧缝抽褶、斜向drape、局部twist比单纯收窄更高级

### 轮廓C：Compact Knit / Sleek Mini（极简紧身）

`
compact jersey mini dress or skirt,
clean minimal lines, matte finish,
high-recovery stretch fabric,
above-knee length, sharp hemline,
NOT embellished, NOT shiny, effortless precision
`

最佳面料：ponte, compact jersey, double-knit
最佳场景：日常·通勤·街拍
关键：面料平整度+边缘处理清爽度+配饰精简度

### 轮廓D：Leather Pencil / Coated Fitted（权力感）

`
leather or coated pencil skirt,
structured rigid fit hugging the hip,
sharp silhouette with clean edges,
NOT soft drape, NOT stretchy flow,
architectural body-contouring
`

最佳面料：leather, coated fabric, structured suiting
最佳场景：职场·酒会·秀场
关键：材质从"软弹"转向"硬挺+贴身"= 从甜辣变成锋利时装
